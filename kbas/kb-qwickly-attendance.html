Taking Attendance with Qwickly Attendance Pro in Canvas

Qwickly Attendance Pro is built into your Canvas course and lets you take attendance in seconds — by roster list, student check-in with PIN, QR code, or Rutgers ID card, or seating chart. Records sync automatically to the Canvas Gradebook when Gradebook integration is enabled.

Category: Engage Your Students · Last reviewed: July 2026 · Reviewed by: ATS, Rutgers–Newark

When to Use This

  • You want attendance records that flow directly into the Canvas Gradebook without manual data entry
  • You teach a large enrollment course and need students to self-check-in rather than calling a roster
  • You want to track participation or late arrivals consistently across the semester
  • You need to send automated absence alerts to students who exceed a threshold
  • You teach hybrid and want a single attendance record covering both in-person and remote students
🔧
Software & Integrations
Qwickly Attendance Pro is installed in Canvas at Rutgers–Newark and RBS at no cost to faculty or students. It is the recommended attendance tool at RU-N and RBS — the built-in Canvas attendance tool has been disabled in favor of Qwickly because it offers significantly more flexibility, better Gradebook integration, and support for large and hybrid courses. Students do not need a separate account — they log in using their Rutgers NetID via SSO.

First-Time Setup

You only need to do this once per course. Setup takes about five minutes.

  1. Enable Qwickly and open Settings — Go to Settings in your Canvas course, then select the Navigation tab. Find Qwickly Attendance in the hidden items list and drag it into the visible navigation. Select Save, then click the Qwickly Attendance link now visible in your course menu — on first launch it opens directly into Settings so you can configure everything below before taking attendance.
  2. Configure your attendance statuses — By default you'll have Present, Absent, and Late. You can add custom statuses (e.g. Excused, Remote) to match your course policy.
  3. Set your Gradebook integration — Choose whether attendance should create a column in the Canvas Gradebook and how it should be weighted. You can change this at any time.
  4. Choose your default attendance method — Select how you'll typically take attendance each session. You can change methods session by session.
  5. Configure absence alerts (optional) — Set a threshold (e.g. 3 absences) to automatically email students when they reach it. Customize the email message to reflect your course policy.
💡 Setup Tip
Do your first-time setup before the semester begins. ATS offers course readiness consultations where a technologist can walk you through Qwickly setup alongside your full Canvas course configuration — schedule one here.

Attendance Taking Methods

Qwickly Attendance Pro supports several methods. Choose the one that fits your course size and teaching context.

📋
Roster List
Standard
Mark each student present, absent, or late from a full class list. You control every entry directly.
Best for: small seminars, discussion-based courses
📱
Student Check-In with PIN
Recommended
You open a session and display a 4-digit PIN on your screen. Students enter it in Canvas or the Qwickly app on their device to mark themselves present.
Best for: large lectures, active learning classrooms
📷
QR Code Check-In
Recommended
Display a QR code on the projector — students scan it with the Qwickly mobile app, and the code rotates every few seconds to prevent sharing. Requires the Qwickly mobile app — cannot be scanned from a browser.
Best for: large courses, fastest and most fraud-resistant check-in
🪪
Rutgers ID Card Check-In
Card Reader Required
Available in some RU-N classrooms — students pass their Rutgers ID card by a reader to check into the course, with no phone or app needed. Faculty interested in this feature should contact ATS.
Best for: classrooms with an installed ID card reader
🗺️
Seating Chart
Visual
Build a seating map of your classroom and mark students by seat position. Useful when you know where students sit regularly.
Best for: courses with assigned or regular seating
📲
Qwickly Mobile App
App Required
Students download the free Qwickly Attendance app (iOS & Android) and log in with their Rutgers NetID. The app enables QR code scanning, location confirmation, and lets students view their own attendance record anytime.
Required for: QR code check-in · Also enables location-based check-in
💡 Large Course Tip
For lectures with 50+ students, PIN or QR code check-in is far faster than calling a roster. Set a check-in timer equal to the length of your class so the session closes automatically — no need to remember to end it manually.

Taking Attendance Each Session

  1. Open Qwickly Attendance — Click Qwickly Attendance in your Canvas course navigation at the start of class.
  2. Click "Take Attendance" — Select your method for this session. You can switch methods from session to session.
  3. For PIN or QR check-in — Display the PIN or QR code to students on your projector or screen. Students open Canvas on their device, navigate to Qwickly Attendance in your course, and enter the PIN or scan the code.
  4. For roster list — Work through the student list and mark each status. You can mark all present first, then adjust for absences.
  5. End the session — Click End Check-In when done, or let the timer close it automatically if you set one. Records save immediately.
  6. Review and edit if needed — Go to Attendance Records at any time to correct any entry. Manual attendance can be backdated; check-in sessions cannot.

RU-N & RBS Workflow
At Rutgers–Newark and RBS, attendance records in Qwickly sync automatically to the Canvas Gradebook when Gradebook integration is enabled. This means attendance is visible to students in their grade summary and flows into your final grade calculation if you weight it. Before the semester begins, confirm your Gradebook integration settings match your syllabus policy — ATS can help during a course setup consultation.

Attendance in Hybrid and Online Courses

Qwickly Attendance Pro supports attendance tracking beyond the physical classroom.

  • Hybrid courses — Take roster attendance for in-person students and use the manual entry or Student Activity mode for remote students attending via Zoom. Both record to the same session.
  • Student Activity mode — Automatically marks students present based on their Canvas activity (viewing pages, submitting assignments, posting to discussions). Useful for fully online or asynchronous courses where "attendance" means engagement.
  • Zoom reminders — Use Canvas Announcements alongside Qwickly to remind remote students to log their attendance before the check-in window closes.
  • Custom statuses — Add a "Remote" or "Attended via Zoom" status to distinguish modality in your records without marking students absent. You can also add a note to any entry with more detail about the absence — for example, the reason a student missed class.

Gradebook Integration and Reports

Qwickly Attendance Pro connects directly to Canvas Gradebook and provides reporting tools that go beyond a simple attendance log.

  • Gradebook column — When enabled, a running attendance score appears in the Canvas Gradebook, visible to students and factored into their grade automatically.
  • Absence alerts — Automated emails to students when they reach a set number of absences. You can customize the message and threshold per course.
  • Attendance reports — Download a CSV of attendance records at any time from the Records section. Useful for grade audits, early alert reporting, or student advising.
  • Student view — Students can see their own attendance record in Qwickly at any time — how many sessions they've attended, missed, or arrived late. This reduces "how many absences do I have?" emails significantly.

RU-N & RBS Attendance Policy
Attendance policies at Rutgers–Newark and RBS are set at the course and department level — there is no university-wide mandatory attendance policy. Your syllabus is the governing document. Make sure your Qwickly configuration (statuses, absence thresholds, Gradebook weighting) matches exactly what your syllabus states. If you are unsure how to align Qwickly settings with your policy, contact ATS before the semester begins.

Setting Up the Qwickly Mobile App for Students

The Qwickly Attendance app is free for students and required if you use QR code check-in. Share these steps with your students at the start of the semester — include them in your syllabus or a first-week Canvas announcement.

  1. Download the app — Students search for Qwickly Attendance in the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android) and install it for free.
  2. Open the app and search for the school — Students tap Find My School and search for Rutgers-Newark. They must select the correct school name — the app will not work if the wrong institution is selected.
  3. Log in with Rutgers NetID — Students tap Login with School SSO and authenticate using their Rutgers NetID and password. Multi-factor authentication (DUO) will be required.
  4. Find the course — Once logged in, students see their active courses. When you open an attendance session, it appears in the app automatically.
  5. Check in — Students tap the open session and either enter the PIN or point their camera at the QR code on your screen to check in.
💡 App Note
Students can also check in via PIN using a web browser in Canvas — the Qwickly app is only required for QR code scanning. If a student has trouble with the app, they can always use the PIN method via Canvas on any device as a fallback.
🔧
Software & Integrations
The Qwickly mobile app uses Rutgers NetID SSO with DUO multi-factor authentication. Students should test the app before the first graded attendance session — some students experience login issues on first use due to MFA prompts. If a student cannot log in, direct them to Rutgers University Help Desk. Faculty should not troubleshoot app login issues directly — this is an IT account issue, not a Canvas issue.

Tips

💡 First Week Tip
Run a practice check-in session during the first class and tell students it won't count. Ask students to download the Qwickly app and log in before class — include the download link and school name (Rutgers-Newark) in your first Canvas announcement. The practice session surfaces login and app issues while there's still time to fix them without affecting grades.
💡 Consistency Tip
Use the same attendance method every session. Switching between PIN, QR, and roster creates confusion for students and inconsistent records. Pick one method at the start of semester and stick to it.
💡 Records Tip
Review your attendance records once a week — not at the end of the semester. Catching and correcting errors while they're fresh is far easier than reconstructing records during final grade submission.
