Using the InstructorCam for Zoom and Web Conferencing

The InstructorCam is a built-in ceiling or podium-mounted camera available in Rutgers–Newark auditoriums and most lecture halls. It connects directly to the classroom computer for use with Zoom, Webex, Teams, or any web conferencing application — no extra hardware required. On the touch panel it is labeled Presenter Camera.

Before You Start

The InstructorCam is only available in rooms that have one installed. Look for this sticker on the podium to confirm your room has an InstructorCam.

InstructorCam Sticker

When to Use the InstructorCam

  • You teach a hybrid course where some students attend in person and others join via Zoom
  • You are holding virtual office hours from a classroom
  • You want to live-stream a lecture, guest speaker, or event
  • You need a high-quality camera angle for a video conference beyond what your laptop webcam provides
  • You want to record your session using Zoom cloud recording

Setting Up the InstructorCam

  1. On the touch panel, tap Presenter Camera — This is the InstructorCam button on the main source selection screen. Tapping it powers on the camera and makes its feed available to the podium computer.
    Tap Presenter Camera on the source selection screen to activate the InstructorCam
  2. Select a camera angle — Choose from preset angles using the touch panel controls:
    • Wide room view — shows the full classroom, good for hybrid teaching
    • Podium close-up — frames you at the podium, good for presentations
    • Whiteboard / board view — points at the board, good for writing-intensive sessions
    Or use the manual pan, tilt, and zoom controls to set a custom angle.
  3. Press Go Back — Return to the main touch panel screen. The InstructorCam stays active.
💡 Setup Tip
Set your camera angle and test it before students arrive. You can also tap Video Conference on the touch panel as an alternative mode for conferencing — it activates the same Presenter Camera but may offer different preset configurations depending on the room.

Using the InstructorCam with Zoom

  1. Open Zoom on the podium computer — Zoom is installed on all RU-N classroom computers. Sign in with your Rutgers NetID via SSO.
  2. Start or join your meeting — Click your scheduled meeting or start a new one.
  3. Select the Presenter Camera as your video source — Click the arrow next to the camera icon in Zoom's toolbar → Select a Camera → choose Presenter Camera or InstructorCam (the device may appear under either name depending on the room). The camera feed replaces your webcam in the meeting.
  4. Select the room microphone as your audio source — Click the arrow next to the microphone icon → Select a Microphone → choose the room microphone. For auditoriums, a lavalier mic is also available — clip it on if you plan to move away from the podium.
  5. Confirm remote participants can see and hear you — Ask a remote student or colleague to confirm your video and audio before class begins.

Recording Your Session in Zoom

You can record directly in Zoom using the room's Presenter Camera and microphone. The recording saves to your Zoom cloud account and can be shared with students via a link.

  1. Confirm the Presenter Camera and room microphone are selected — Complete the setup steps above before starting your recording. The recording captures whatever camera and audio source Zoom is currently using.
  2. Click Record in Zoom — In the Zoom toolbar, click Record → Record to the Cloud. A recording indicator appears. Cloud recording is recommended over local recording so the file is accessible from any device after class.
  3. Teach your class — Zoom records continuously in the background. You can pause and resume using the recording controls in the toolbar.
  4. Click Stop Recording when finished — Or end the meeting — Zoom stops the recording automatically. The file processes and uploads to your Zoom cloud account, which may take a few minutes.
  5. Share the recording link with students — Log in to rutgers.zoom.us, go to Recordings, and copy the shareable link. Paste it into a Canvas Announcement or Page so students can access it.
🔧
Software & Integrations
Zoom cloud recording is available to all Rutgers faculty via rutgers.zoom.us. Log in with your NetID to access your recordings. Cloud recordings are retained for 180 days by default — download the file or save it to Kaltura before it expires if you want to keep it longer.

Tips for Hybrid Teaching

💡 Camera Angle
Use a wide preset angle for most of your hybrid class so remote students can see the full room environment. Switch to a tighter podium angle when presenting slides, and a board-facing angle when writing. Camera presets are labeled on the touch panel and vary slightly by room — explore them before your first class. Tell remote students when you switch angles.
💡 Audio
Repeat questions from in-person students before answering — remote participants cannot always hear audience questions, even with room microphones. This improves the experience for everyone and is good pedagogical practice regardless of technology.
💡 Before First Class
Run a full test session — camera, audio, Zoom — before your first hybrid class with actual students. ATS can join your test session remotely to confirm everything is working from the student side. Request a test session here.

Troubleshooting

Problem
What to do
Presenter Camera does not appear in Zoom's camera list
Confirm you tapped Presenter Camera on the touch panel first — the camera must be activated there before it appears as a device in Zoom. Then in Zoom, click the camera arrow and refresh the device list. If still missing, close and reopen Zoom.
Remote participants can't hear you
In Zoom's audio settings, confirm the room microphone is selected as the input — not the computer's built-in mic. Check the touch panel to ensure the room mic is not muted. For auditoriums, try the lavalier mic as an alternative.
Camera image is dark or out of focus
Use the touch panel to switch camera presets or manually adjust. If the image is very dark, the room lighting may be too low — raise the lights using the room controls. If the camera image looks wrong after adjustments, call ATS at 973-353-1713.
Zoom shows your laptop webcam instead of InstructorCam
You may have joined Zoom from your laptop instead of the podium computer. Use the podium computer for InstructorCam access — it cannot be shared to a laptop over the display connection.
InstructorCam sticker is on the podium but camera is not working
Call ATS immediately at 973-353-1713 with your building and room number. Do not attempt to reconnect or adjust physical camera hardware yourself.
