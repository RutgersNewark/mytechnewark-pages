Teaching in Our Classrooms

Teaching in
Digital Classrooms

Digital Classrooms at Rutgers–Newark are designed to support flexible, technology-enhanced teaching in small and medium-sized learning spaces.

These classrooms support in-person, hybrid, and multimedia instruction with integrated presentation systems, classroom audio, and teaching tools.

Getting Started in Your Digital Classroom

Follow these simple steps to get your class started.

1
Turn on the podium computer
2
Log in with your NetID
3
Wake the touch control panel
4
Select your presentation source
5
Share content with your class
Use the touch control panel to manage sources, displays, audio, and more.

InstructorCam & Hybrid Teaching

Use InstructorCam for video conferencing, hybrid instruction, lecture recordings, and remote participation.

  • Zoom, Teams, Webex, and more
  • Wide room coverage
  • Manual camera control
  • Guest speaker views
Learn About InstructorCam

Classroom Capture

Record and share class sessions with Kaltura Capture.

  • Record lectures and presentations
  • Capture audio, video, and content
  • Create review materials for students
  • Access in MediaSpace
Learn About Capture

EchoPoll

Use live polling and audience response to check comprehension and boost participation during class.

  • Launch polls from any device
  • Real-time student responses
  • Word clouds, quizzes, and surveys
  • Results visible on classroom display
  • Integrates with Canvas gradebook
Learn About EchoPoll
