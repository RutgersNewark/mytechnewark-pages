Using Akindi for Paper-Based Assessments in Canvas

How To Assess & Grade Akindi Canvas Multiple Choice Bubble Sheet Grading

Akindi is a web-based assessment tool that grades multiple-choice tests using regular paper and any scanner — no Scantron machine required. It is available to all Rutgers faculty through Canvas and syncs grades directly to the Canvas Gradebook.

Quick Facts

ToolAkindi
Best forPaper multiple-choice quizzes and exams
Works withCanvas — grades sync automatically
You needA printer, a scanner or copier with document feeder, PDF upload access
WorkflowPrint → Scan → Upload

Overview

Akindi replaces the traditional Scantron system with a more flexible, fully digital workflow. Instead of proprietary answer sheets and expensive scanning machines, Akindi uses regular 8.5" × 11" paper and any printer or scanner — including departmental copiers.

It is well suited for:

  • In-person quizzes and exams with multiple-choice questions
  • Large enrollment courses where hand-grading is not practical
  • Instructors who want instant grade analytics after an exam
  • Courses where up to six different exam versions are needed

What Akindi Does

  • Creates paper assessments with custom multiple-choice questions
  • Prints custom bubble sheets — including pre-filled student names and RU IDs
  • Grades completed sheets automatically after scanning
  • Flags unclear or incomplete bubbles for manual review
  • Sends grades directly to the Canvas Gradebook
  • Emails individual corrected sheets to students
  • Provides item analysis — showing which questions were hardest and how well each question discriminated between high and low performers
  • Archives scanned sheet images digitally so paper copies do not need to be retained

Quick Start

Akindi follows a three-step workflow from assessment to grades in Canvas.

1
Print
Create assessment in Akindi. Set your answer key and print bubble sheets for your class.
2
Scan
Collect and scan completed sheets into a single PDF using any scanner or copier.
3
Upload
Upload PDF to Akindi. Sheets are graded instantly. Review exceptions and send grades to Canvas.

Before You Begin

Confirm you have the following before your first Akindi assessment:

  • An active Rutgers Canvas course with your students enrolled
  • Akindi enabled in your course navigation (see next section)
  • Access to a printer to print bubble sheets
  • Access to a scanner or copier with a document feeder that can produce a PDF
  • Your answer key finalized before or shortly after administering the assessment
Pre-Print Tip Akindi pre-prints bubble sheets with student names and RU IDs from your Canvas roster. This prevents matching errors at grade time and is strongly recommended — especially for courses where multiple student ID systems are in use at Rutgers.

Enable Akindi in Canvas

Akindi must be added to your course navigation before you can use it. This is a one-time setup per course.

  1. 1
    Open your Canvas course — Log in at rutgers.instructure.com and navigate to your course.
  2. 2
    Go to Settings — Select Settings in the course navigation menu.
  3. 3
    Open the Navigation tab — At the top of the Settings page, select the Navigation tab.
  4. 4
    Enable Akindi — Find Akindi in the list of hidden items at the bottom of the page. Drag it into the visible navigation section, or select the options menu and choose Enable.
  5. 5
    Save — Select Save at the bottom of the Navigation tab.
  6. 6
    Launch Akindi — Select Akindi in your course navigation. Akindi opens in a new tab and automatically syncs your Canvas course roster.

How to Use Akindi

Create an Assessment

  1. 1
    Select "Create Assessment" — In the Akindi dashboard, select Create Assessment in the upper right.
  2. 2
    Name the assessment — Enter a clear name that includes the course and date (e.g., PSYCH 101 — Midterm — Oct 14).
  3. 3
    Select paper-based assessment — Choose the Question & Bubble Sheet workflow for a standard paper exam.
  4. 4
    Add questions and set the answer key — Enter questions manually or import from a Word (.docx) file. In the Word file, bold the correct answer and Akindi sets the key automatically. The answer key can be edited at any time — even after sheets have been scanned.
  5. 5
    Choose whether to prefill student information — Enable pre-printing of student names and RU IDs. Strongly recommended to prevent grade matching errors.

Print Answer Sheets

  1. 1
    Select "Print Bubble Sheets" — Akindi generates a PDF of answer sheets ready to print.
  2. 2
    Print enough copies for your class — Print one sheet per student. If running multiple exam versions, Akindi includes version bubbles on each sheet.
  3. 3
    Print an extra copy for your answer key — Alternatively, set your answer key digitally in Akindi before the exam.
Print Quality Tip Make sure QR codes and the corners of each bubble sheet print clearly — Akindi uses these to orient and identify sheets during scanning. Print a single test sheet first and confirm it is legible before printing the full class set.

Administer the Assessment

  1. 1
    Distribute the answer sheets — Hand out one sheet per student just like a traditional Scantron sheet.
  2. 2
    Students complete responses — Students can use any writing utensil — pencil or pen of any color. Remind them to fill bubbles clearly and fully.
  3. 3
    Collect sheets carefully — Keep completed sheets flat and avoid folding or creasing. Damaged corners or obscured QR codes can cause scanning exceptions.

Scan the Answer Sheets

  1. 1
    Load sheets into the document feeder — Use any scanner or departmental copier with a document feeder. Scan all completed sheets for one assessment into a single PDF file.
  2. 2
    Scan in the same orientation — Keep all sheets face-up and aligned in the same direction. Akindi can correct upside-down sheets automatically, but consistent orientation produces the cleanest results.
  3. 3
    Save the PDF — Save or email the PDF to yourself before uploading. Do not upload directly from the copier without saving a copy first.
iOS Option The Akindi iOS app lets you scan answer sheets using your phone camera if a scanner is not available. This is useful for small classes or urgent situations — though a document-feeder scanner produces the most reliable results for large batches.

Upload and Review

  1. 1
    Upload the PDF to Akindi — In your Akindi assessment, select Upload Sheets and choose your PDF. Alternatively, email the PDF to your unique Akindi assessment email address — each assessment has one listed in its settings.
  2. 2
    Wait for processing — Akindi grades all sheets immediately after upload. A notification confirms when processing is complete.
  3. 3
    Review the "Review & Fix" tab — Any sheets with missing, unclear, or ambiguous bubbles appear here. Akindi shows you a cropped image of the problematic area and lets you correct it manually without rescanning.
  4. 4
    Confirm scoring — Check the Overview tab for average scores, question difficulty, and discriminatory scores. Adjust the answer key if needed — Akindi will regrade automatically.

Send Grades to Canvas

  1. 1
    Select "Send Grades to LMS" — When satisfied with the results, select Send Grades to Canvas.
  2. 2
    Select the correct Gradebook item — Choose the Canvas assignment you want the grades applied to. If no assignment exists yet, create it in Canvas first.
  3. 3
    Confirm — Grades appear in the Canvas Gradebook within a few minutes.

Additional export options available after grading:

  • Download a responses CSV with all student grades
  • Download a question breakdown CSV for item analysis
  • Email students a secure link to their individual corrected sheet
  • Download a PDF of all corrected student reports

RU-N & RBS Scanning Workflow

Using Departmental Copiers at Rutgers–Newark and RBS Most buildings have departmental copiers with scan-to-email capability. Recommended workflow:
  1. Load your completed answer sheets into the copier document feeder
  2. Use the copier's Scan to Email function to send the PDF to your Rutgers email address
  3. Save the PDF from your email to your computer before uploading to Akindi
  4. Keep the saved PDF as a backup until grades are confirmed in Canvas

If you are unsure which copier to use or need help with scan-to-email setup, contact ATS or visit Blumenthal Hall, Room 100.

Best Practices

🖨️
Test print one sheet first

Print and scan a single sample before your exam day to confirm print quality, QR code clarity, and that Akindi reads it correctly.

🎓
Pre-print with student names and IDs

Eliminates manual matching and prevents grade sync errors, especially in courses with large rosters.

📄
Scan all sheets in the same orientation

Face-up, same direction. Akindi handles upside-down sheets but consistency reduces exceptions.

📁
Use one combined PDF per assessment

Do not upload multiple partial PDFs for the same exam — combine all sheets into a single file before uploading.

✅
Review exceptions before sending grades

Always check the Review & Fix tab and confirm scores look correct before pushing grades to Canvas.

🔑
Adjust the answer key before sending grades

If you need to drop a question or accept multiple correct answers, do it in Akindi first — it will regrade automatically.

Troubleshooting

Problem What to Do
Akindi is not visible in Canvas navigation Go to Course Settings → Navigation tab and enable Akindi from the hidden items list. If Akindi does not appear at all, contact your campus support — it may need to be enabled at the course or account level.
Students are missing from the Akindi roster Akindi syncs from Canvas on launch. Close and reopen Akindi from your Canvas course to trigger a fresh sync. If students are still missing, confirm they are fully enrolled — pending or inactive enrollments will not sync.
PDF upload fails or times out Check the file size — very large PDFs (100+ pages) may time out on slower connections. Try the email upload method instead: email the PDF to your unique Akindi assessment address. Confirm the PDF is not password-protected.
Sheets are not recognized or show many exceptions Check print quality — QR codes and sheet corners must be clean and unobstructed. Sheets that were folded, wet, or scanned at very low resolution (below 200 DPI) may not process correctly. Re-scan at a higher resolution if possible.
Grades do not appear in Canvas after sending Confirm you selected the correct Canvas assignment in the grade sync step. If the assignment does not exist in Canvas yet, create it first then return to Akindi and send grades again. Grades can take a few minutes to appear — refresh the Canvas Gradebook before troubleshooting further.
Grades sent to the wrong Canvas course Always confirm you are in the correct Akindi assessment before sending grades. Contact ATS if grades need to be moved between courses.
