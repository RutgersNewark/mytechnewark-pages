Campus platforms and digital services

This page brings together Newark-supported services that help departments, offices, student organizations, and the campus community manage events, promote programs, reserve spaces, and share information.

These tools are part of a broader technology enablement strategy: connecting people with the right platforms, reducing confusion about where to go, and making campus processes easier to navigate.

Whether you are planning an event, communicating with students, managing a student organization, or sharing information across campus, these services help turn everyday operational needs into clear, supported digital workflows.
