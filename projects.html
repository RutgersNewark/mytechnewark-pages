Rutgers–Newark IT manages a broad portfolio of projects, programs, pilots, and technology initiatives that support teaching, learning, student success, campus operations, and digital engagement.

This page highlights selected strategic initiatives and provides a searchable view of current, ongoing, and completed work across the Rutgers–Newark technology ecosystem.

Featured Projects

High-priority, high-visibility initiatives currently underway.

In Progress

RaiderLink Expansion

RaiderLink is Rutgers–Newark's student engagement platform, supporting events, student organizations, orientation activities, checklists, elections, and campus engagement workflows.

  • Area: Student Engagement & Success
  • Audience: Students, Staff, Campus Partners
  • Impact: Expands the campus's ability to connect students with opportunities, services, organizations, and timely communications.
In Progress

RaiderBot

AI-powered student support chatbot designed to help current students find answers and connect with the right services.

In Progress

AI Fluency Program

The AI Fluency Program helps students build practical, responsible, and creative AI skills through a badge-based learning experience. The program introduces foundational AI concepts, responsible use, productivity tools, and creative applications.

  • Area: AI & Digital Fluency
  • Audience: Students
  • Impact: Supports student readiness for academic, professional, and creative uses of AI.
In Progress

Wix Enterprise Web Modernization

Rutgers–Newark is modernizing websites for schools, centers, and programs through a shared Wix Enterprise platform. This effort supports improved design, accessibility, analytics, content workflows, and long-term sustainability.

  • Area: Web & Digital Experience
  • Audience: Schools, Centers, Programs, Campus Partners
  • Impact: Helps campus units maintain modern, effective websites that better support enrollment, communications, and service delivery.
In Progress

Course Materials Access Project

Work to improve course materials visibility, affordability, inclusive access, and integration with Canvas and student systems.

Ongoing Program

Classroom Modernization Program

Rutgers–Newark has made a sustained, campus-wide investment in modernizing centrally managed classrooms and learning spaces to support contemporary teaching, digital learning, student engagement, and instructional innovation.

  • Area: Learning Environments
  • Audience: Faculty, Students, Academic Departments
  • Impact: Improves classroom reliability, instructional technology, wireless connectivity, active learning, hybrid teaching capabilities, and the overall student learning experience.
  • Last Updated: July 2026
