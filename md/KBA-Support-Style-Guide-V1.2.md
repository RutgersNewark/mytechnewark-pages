# MyTech Knowledge Base Style Guide v1.2
**myTech@Newark — Innovation at Rutgers University–Newark**

---

## Purpose

The MyTech Knowledge Base uses a standardized design system to help users quickly determine:
- Is this article for me?
- How do I complete the task or understand the service?
- If I still need help, who should I contact?

Every article should follow a consistent structure and use the approved audience and support routing patterns. Support information should appear after the article content so users are encouraged to read the KBA before contacting support.

---

## Standard Article Structure

Every KBA should contain:

1. Identity Bar
2. Audience Banner
3. Article Header
4. Overview
5. Content (steps, screenshots, callouts, troubleshooting)
6. Related Articles
7. Support Routing Block
8. Brand Footer

Support routing blocks appear near the bottom of the article, after users have had an opportunity to read the instructions and review troubleshooting guidance.

---

## Identity Bar

The Identity Bar appears at the top of every article and establishes the Rutgers University–Newark brand context.

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Logo | Official Rutgers–Newark horizontal logo |
| Site name | **myTech@Newark Knowledge Base** |
| Background | White or very light neutral |
| Text | Rutgers black or navy |
| Logo usage | Use the official Rutgers–Newark logo asset. Do not recreate the logo with text, use an unofficial block R, or substitute another Rutgers mark. |

The Identity Bar should make it clear that the article belongs to the Rutgers University–Newark myTech@Newark knowledge base. The tagline should not be required in the Identity Bar; reserve the tagline for the Brand Footer.

## Support Routing Framework

Support routing answers: **Who should I contact if the article does not resolve my issue?**

Support routing blocks should appear near the bottom of each article, after the main content, troubleshooting, and related articles. This placement encourages users to read and use the KBA first before contacting support.

Use the phrase **Still Need Help?** as the section heading immediately above the support routing block.

## Brand Footer

The Brand Footer appears at the bottom of every article after the Support Routing Block.

The footer is for branding only. Do not include phone numbers, email addresses, office locations, ticket links, or support routing instructions in the footer. Support contact information belongs only in the Support Routing Block.

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Logo | Official Rutgers–Newark horizontal logo |
| Brand | **myTech@Newark** |
| Tagline | *Innovation at Rutgers University–Newark* |
| Background | White or light neutral |
| Text | Rutgers black or navy |

Recommended footer content:

**[Official Rutgers–Newark Logo]**

**myTech@Newark**  
*Innovation at Rutgers University–Newark*
