# MyTech Knowledge Base Style Guide v1.1
**myTech@Newark — Innovation at Rutgers University–Newark**

---

## Purpose

The MyTech Knowledge Base uses a standardized design system to help users quickly determine:
- Is this article for me?
- Who supports this service?
- Where do I go for help?

Every article should follow a consistent structure and use the approved audience and support routing patterns.

---

## Standard Article Structure

Every KBA should contain:

1. Identity Bar
2. Audience Banner
3. Article Header
4. Overview
5. Content (steps, screenshots, callouts, troubleshooting)
6. Support Routing Block
7. Related Articles

---

## Identity Bar

The Identity Bar appears at the top of every article and establishes the brand context.

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Site name | **myTech@Newark** |
| Tagline | *Innovation at Rutgers University–Newark* |
| Background | Navy `#1B3A6B` |
| Text | White `#FFFFFF` |

The tagline **"myTech@Newark — Innovation at Rutgers University–Newark"** must appear in full in the Identity Bar of every article. Do not abbreviate or omit it.
